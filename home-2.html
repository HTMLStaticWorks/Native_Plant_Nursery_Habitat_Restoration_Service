Our Restoration Process

How we turn degraded landscapes into thriving habitats.

1

Site Assessment

We analyze soil composition, hydrology, sunlight patterns, and existing invasive species to understand the ecological baseline.

2

Ecological Planning

Designing a plant community blueprint tailored to the specific ecoregion, maximizing biodiversity and succession patterns.

3

Site Preparation

Careful removal of invasive species and preparation of the seedbed using sustainable, chemical-free methods where possible.

4

Native Plant Installation

Installing high-quality native plugs, seeds, and container plants sourced from specific local genetics.

5

Monitoring & Maintenance

Ongoing stewardship to manage weed competition and ensure successful establishment of the native ecosystem.

The Ripple Effect of Native Habitats

When you choose native plants, you aren't just landscaping—you are creating a sanctuary. From specialized bees to migratory birds, local wildlife depends entirely on the plants that co-evolved in their region.

Pollinators

Supports butterflies, moths, and native bees.

Avian Life
