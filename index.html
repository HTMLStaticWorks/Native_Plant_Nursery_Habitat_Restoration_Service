Why Native Plants Matter

Native plants are the ecological basis upon which life depends, including birds and people. Without them and the insects that co-evolved with them, local food webs collapse.

Water Conservation

Once established, native plants require significantly less water, surviving on natural rainfall.

Pollinator Support

They provide the exact nectar and pollen that local bees, butterflies, and insects need.

Climate Resilience

Deep root systems store carbon, stabilize soil, and resist extreme weather conditions.
