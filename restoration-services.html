Five-Phase Scientific Workflow

Our Restoration Process

A rigorous, biologically-backed methodology ensuring self-sustaining ecological recovery.

1 Phase 01

Site
Assessment

In-depth soil microbiology profiling, hydrology flow modeling, and invasive seed-bank audits to establish an ecological baseline.

2 Phase 02

Blueprint
Design

Developing site-specific genotype palettes, root architecture zoning, and four-season pollinator succession schedules.

3 Phase 03

Site
Preparation

Low-disturbance removal of persistent invasives, bioswale contouring, and biological microbial soil inoculation.

4 Phase 04

Native
Planting

Precision installation of nursery-grown, neonicotinoid-free deep-plug wildflowers, native grasses, and bare-root trees.

5 Phase 05

Monitoring &
Maintenance

Multi-season botanical health audits, spot weed stewardship, and our 3-year establishment guarantee for total success.
