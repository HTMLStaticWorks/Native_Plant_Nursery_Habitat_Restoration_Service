Choose the Right Native Plants

Match plants to your specific site conditions for ecological success.

Sun Loving

Requires 6+ hours of direct sunlight. Ideal for prairie and meadow species.

Shade Tolerant

Thrives in less than 4 hours of sun. Perfect for woodland gardens and ferns.

Dry Soil

Deep-rooted species that excel in well-drained, sandy, or rocky conditions.

Moist Soil

Plants for rain gardens, low spots, and clay soils that hold water.

Pollinator Gardens

High nectar output flowers blooming in succession to feed insects.

Bird Habitat

Shrubs and trees offering berries, nesting sites, and caterpillars.

Step-by-Step Planting Guide

Follow these steps to give your native plants the best start.

1

Prepare the Site

Remove invasive species and lawn grass. Do not till the soil, as this brings weed seeds to the surface.

2

Select Species

Choose plants appropriate for your light and soil conditions. Avoid amending the soil; native plants prefer native soil.

3

Dig the Hole

Dig a hole twice as wide as the pot but no deeper. The root flare should sit slightly above ground level.

4

Plant Correctly

Gently loosen the roots. Place in hole and backfill with the original soil. Tamp down lightly to remove air pockets.

5

Water Properly

Water deeply immediately after planting. Continue watering during dry spells for the first year until established.

6

Maintain & Monitor

Mulch lightly with natural leaf litter or hardwood mulch (keep away from stems). Pull weeds as needed.

Seasonal Planting Calendar

When to plant and when to plan.

Spring
Excellent time for planting container plants and bare root trees. Ensure danger of hard frost has passed. Early spring is also ideal for sowing warm-season grasses.
Summer
Generally avoid planting during heat waves. If you must plant, rigorous watering is required. Good time for planning fall restorations and site preparation.
Fall
Often the best time to plant! Cooler temps and rain help roots establish before winter. Ideal for dormant seeding of wildflowers.
Winter
Time for "winter sowing" seeds that require cold stratification. Excellent time for pruning dormant native trees and shrubs.
